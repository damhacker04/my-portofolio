## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** International, remote-first tech recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Kaesar
Adam Rafano for junior-to-mid **front-end / web developer** roles. They arrive from a
LinkedIn link, a job application, or a referral and scan the portfolio quickly — often on
desktop, sometimes mobile — to decide within a minute or two whether he is worth an
interview. They read in English and look for evidence of real, shipped work rather than
tutorials or coursework.

**Secondary (not the design target, but welcome):** local startups/agencies and freelance
clients who may find him through the same site.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio for Kaesar Adam Rafano. It exists to convert a recruiter's
short scan into a reply — an interview request or a message — by proving he already ships
real software. Success is a recruiter taking him seriously and reaching out, despite him
still being a student.

## Positioning

A **front-end–focused web developer who has already shipped real, deployed production
projects as a student.** Front-end is the craft he is deliberately committing to and
deepening; his proof of strength is breadth of *actual shipping* across web, mobile, and
AI-integrated systems — not depth-of-expertise claims. Backend and system-analysis work are
recorded honestly as **exploration**, not mastery.

The rare, truthful edge a neighboring portfolio cannot copy: multiple genuinely deployed
products (several with live URLs and public repos) built and shipped **before graduation**,
including AI-integrated systems — evidence of shipping, not just learning.

## Operating Context

- The recruiter opens one URL and scans top-to-bottom: hero → proof/projects → experience →
  contact, usually inside 60–120 seconds before deciding.
- Single-page site, English only. Read on both desktop and mobile; light and dark mode.
- The decision is comparative — this portfolio sits in a stack of many candidates, so the
  first viewport and the project evidence carry most of the weight.

## Product Principles

1. **Honesty over hype.** Front-end is the chosen direction; backend/SA are exploration.
   Never manufacture seniority the evidence doesn't support.
2. **Proof of shipping leads.** Real, deployed products — not coursework — are the core
   argument. Lead with what a recruiter can click and verify.
3. **Designed for a 60-second international scan.** English, scannable, fast, and legible on
   both desktop and mobile; the first viewport must earn the next scroll.
4. **Copy must not sound machine-written.** Natural phrasing; no em-dash/colon/arrow/plus
   punctuation tics in body copy (standing user preference).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Light and dark themes are supported and must both remain legible.
- Must read well on mobile and desktop. No formal standard (e.g. WCAG level) has been
  mandated yet; treat readable contrast and keyboard-reachable controls as the working floor.
